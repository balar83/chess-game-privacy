Privacy Policy — Chess Game + Anti-Chess

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Chess Game + Anti-Chess ("the app") is a chess application offering standard Chess (against the computer) and the Anti-chess variant (against the computer or a second player on the same device). This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No accounts, no personal data collection

The app has no sign-up, login, or user account system. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

What the app sends over the network

When you play Chess against the computer, the app sends the current board position (in standard chess FEN notation) and the selected difficulty level to a backend server so it can calculate the computer's next move. This data describes only the state of the chess game in progress — it contains no personal or device-identifying information, and it is not linked to you or stored against any user identity. Anti-chess is played entirely on your device — its computer opponent runs locally and never contacts the network.

Server logs

Like most web services, the backend server that computes computer moves may briefly process your device's IP address as part of standard network request handling and abuse-prevention (rate limiting). This is not linked to any personal identity, is not used for tracking or profiling, and is not shared with any third party.

Local game save, no cloud or account persistence

The app saves your current in-progress Chess game and your current in-progress Anti-chess game to your device only, so you can pick up where you left off after closing or switching apps. Each save contains the moves played, the game settings you chose (side, difficulty, opponent) and the board orientation. These saves live entirely on your device — they are never sent to a server, never stored in the cloud, and never linked to any account. Starting a new game or finishing a game clears the corresponding save, and uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all saved data. Sound on/off is not stored between launches.

Third-party sharing

The app does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers integrated into the app.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds accounts, persistence, or analytics — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
